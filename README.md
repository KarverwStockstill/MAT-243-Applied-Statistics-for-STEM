# MAT-243-Applied-Statistics-for-STEM
Applied Statistics for STEM

Applying statistical concepts such as descriptive statistics, confidence intervals, p-values, and population versus sample parameters to analyze historical NBA sports data. I used measures including mean, median, and standard deviation, along with tables and charts, to compare NBA teams based on wins, average points scored, relative skill, point differential, and relative point differential. I also used predictive statistical methods, including simple and multiple linear regression, correlation, and scatterplots, to estimate regular-season wins and identify patterns relevant to organizational questions.
